System Maintenance

Perform system power operations on the UPS network adapter.

Restart System

Safely reboot the Raspberry Pi. The SNMP daemon, PeaNUT dashboard, and configuration web interface will restart automatically once the device boots up.

Estimated downtime: 30–60 seconds.
Shutdown System

Safely shut down the operating system and stop all services to prevent SD card corruption before disconnecting power.

Notice: Once shut down, the adapter cannot be turned on remotely over the network. You will need to physically disconnect and reconnect the power cable to power it back on.
